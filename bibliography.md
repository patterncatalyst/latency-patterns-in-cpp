This guide's chapter arc, coverage, and level of rigor draw on a small set of
reference works. They are reference works, not sources: no text or code from
any of them is reproduced here. Every example in this guide is designed from
the technique and measured on its own running domain, a parcel sortation
line, in original C++23 (and, in three chapters, Rust).

|---|---|---|---|---|
